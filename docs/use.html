How to use

Everything Wisp does, and how.

Wisp 1.0 on the Car Thing, part by part. Not set up yet? Start with the setup guide.

01The knob and buttons

ControlWhat it does
Turn the knobMoves through whatever's on screen. On the player and while driving, it's the volume.
Press the knobPicks what it's on. On the player, play and pause.
Back (on the front, beside the knob)One step back.
The top-right buttonPress for the power menu: sleep, restart and more. Press twice for the clock and weather screensaver.
Buttons 1 to 4 (along the top)Your shortcuts: a playlist, the map, Home, the voice, or whatever you set in Settings → Top buttons. Hold one to save what's playing to it.
The screenTouch works too, for the moments it's quicker.

02Your music

Home is the bar along the bottom: Recents, Liked Songs, DJ, your playlists, albums and artists, and doorways to the map, Drive Mode, Add-ons and Settings. Turn to move along it, press to open, press again to play.

  • Spotify plays on the Car Thing itself once you've signed in: play anything on your account from any device and it shows up.
  • YouTube Music and SoundCloud play through your phone, with their libraries on the Car Thing. Sign in from the Wisp app (YouTube Music with a code at google.com/device).
  • Anything else playing on your phone (a podcast app, VLC) shows on the player with its controls.

03The player

The cover, the song and synced lyrics beside it. The knob is the volume and a press plays or pauses.

Tap More on the player for its menu: like, shuffle, repeat, Up next, the visualizer, lyrics on or off, and where it's playing.

Lyrics follow the song line by line, for Spotify and what else your phone plays. If they're early or late, Settings → Lyric sync nudges them.

04Talking to Wisp

Say "Okay Wisp", or press the voice button, then ask the way you'd ask a person. The words show along the bottom, and Wisp answers on screen and out loud (Settings → Wisp's voice).

TryWhat happens
"Play my road trip playlist" · "I wanna hear Daft Punk"Plays it, from your library first.
"Play the album" · "no, the other one"Follows on from what you just asked.
"Queue Midnight City" · "like this" · "add this to my gym playlist"Up next, Liked Songs, a playlist.
"Who sings this?" · "what's the weather?"Answers.
"Take me to the beach" · "when do we get there?" · "stop navigation"Directions (see below).
"Call Mom"Calls through your phone (allow "Call by voice" in the Wisp app).
"I'm getting sleepy" · "dim the screen" · "start Drive Mode"Music to wake you, the screen, Drive Mode.

Everyday requests are understood on the Car Thing itself. With the Wisp app, a language model on your phone handles looser ones: Gemini Nano where your phone has it, or a free open model you can download (Wisp offers it once, and it's in the app under Options → Wisp's thinking).

05Directions

Two ways, picked in Settings → Navigation → Directions by:

  • Google Maps: start directions on your phone and the turns show on the Car Thing's map, with your route.
  • Wisp: the Car Thing finds the place, plans the route and says the turns itself, from OpenStreetMap. Your phone just shares where you are.

06Drive Mode beta

Music that follows the drive: it opens up as you pull away, settles when you coast and goes quiet at a stop. Open it from Home, or say "start Drive Mode", and turn the knob to pick a sound: Ethereal, Cinematic or Lo-fi.

It plays from your phone, which downloads each sound once (from the Wisp app's Drive Mode page).

07Calls and the phone

  • Calls: who's calling, over whatever's on screen. Turn to Answer or Decline and press. Back hides it and leaves the phone ringing. The car's hands-free does the talking.
  • Notifications slide in at the top (Settings → Phone → Notifications).
  • Your phone's battery shows when it isn't charging, and warns you when it's low.

08Add-ons

Home → Add-ons lists yours; press one to open it full screen, where the knob works it and back leaves. Get add-ons is the community's catalog: each with its picture, who made it, what it does and what it uses. Press to install, press again to remove.

To start: a forecast, a big clock, trip gauges, an odometer, a parking timer, sun and moon times, world clocks and a license plate game. Make your own in the Add-on Studio; here's how.

09Settings and updates

Home → Settings: brightness and auto brightness, night mode at sunset, accent colour, performance mode, lyrics, volume per turn, the top buttons, Wisp's voice, navigation, notifications and more. Your phone keeps a copy, so a reset or reflash doesn't lose them.

Updates come over the air: Settings → Software update, or automatically if you allow it. When nothing's playing, the screensaver shows the clock and the weather.

10The Wisp app

On your Android phone, the Wisp app is what brings directions, calls, notifications, YouTube Music and SoundCloud, Drive Mode, the phone's battery and Wisp's spoken voice to the Car Thing. Its setup list shows what's left to allow, and each step says what it's for.

  • Car Thing settings from your phone, changed on the Car Thing right away.
  • Wisp's thinking: which language model helps the voice (Gemini Nano, or the open model).
  • Music services: sign in to YouTube Music and SoundCloud.
