Wisp 1.0

The Car Thing finds its own way now: directions on the dash with turns spoken aloud, add-ons anyone can make, and a voice that understands the way you talk.

How to update New? Set up Wisp
Directions

Wisp's own turn-by-turn

Pick gas, food, coffee, parking, charging or groceries nearby, or say "take me to…". Choose from up to three routes, avoid highways or tolls, and hear every turn, or send the trip to Google Maps for live traffic.

How directions work ›
Map

A new map screen

A big turn card with the turn after it, your speed, and the time, miles and arrival along the bottom. Spoken directions: every turn, just the turns, or muted.

See it ›
Add-ons

Add-ons, with pictures

Eight to start: a forecast, a big clock, trip gauges, an odometer, a parking timer, sun and moon, world clocks and a license plate game. Each shows its picture, who made it and what it does.

Using add-ons ›
Make one

The Add-on Studio

Build an add-on in your browser from tiles, no code needed, or start from a template. Try it on a Car Thing screen with a working knob, then send it in.

Open the Studio ›

Also in 1.0

  • A smarter voice. "I wanna hear Daft Punk", "who sings this?", "play the album", "call Mom", "I'm getting sleepy". Everyday requests are understood on the Car Thing; a model on your phone handles looser ones.
  • Wisp OS. Wisp's own modern system: a current Linux kernel and a GPU-drawn screen. Flash it once; updates come over the air.
  • New Drive Mode music: Ethereal, Cinematic and Lo-fi, remade.
  • YouTube Music signs in with a code at google.com/device.
  • Open source, with a how-to for everything.
