Wisp 1.0.6

The Car Thing finds your phone again by itself: when the car starts, and after a drop.

How to update What came in 1.0.5

Fixed

  • No more "pick a connection" when the car starts. The Car Thing could ask for your paired phone before its Bluetooth was ready, then act as if it had never met it. It now asks again, and says "Reconnecting…" while it does.
  • Reconnects after the phone drops the link. Phones sometimes end the Bluetooth link by themselves to save power. The Car Thing used to take that as you disconnecting and wait 10 minutes; now it tries again after a minute.
  • Clearer setup on Samsung phones. The Wisp app only asks about "Never sleeping apps" when it isn't already allowed to run in the background.
