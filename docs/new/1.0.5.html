Wisp 1.0.5

Pick up where you left off, see every update coming, and talk to Wisp over the music.

How to update What came in 1.0.4

New

  • Pick up where you left off. With nothing playing, the Car Thing shows the last song you played with a Resume button, buttons to choose which phone or speaker plays it, and a reminder that the back button goes Home.
  • Quieter music while Wisp listens. After "Okay Wisp" the music dips (or pauses) until Wisp has your request, so it hears you. Choose in Settings → Wisp's voice → Music while listening. Needs the Wisp app on your phone.
  • Tap off Wisp to close it. A tap anywhere above Wisp's bar closes it at once and stops it listening.
  • "Play my liked songs", "shuffle my likes", "play my Discover Weekly", Release Radar, your Daily Mixes and On Repeat all work on the Car Thing by itself.

Changed

  • Updates ask first again. A new Wisp shows its card as soon as it's out, even while music plays, so you see what's new. Tick "Download and install updates automatically" on the card and later ones install by themselves.
  • A cloud by the battery pulses while an update downloads in the background.
