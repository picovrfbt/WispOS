What's new · October 18, 2026

Wisp 1.0.3

Ask Wisp anything and the model on your phone answers again, and Wisp talks back from the start.

How to update What came in 1.0.2

Fixed

  • Wisp's thinking gets ready. The Wisp app could stay on "Getting Gemini Nano ready…" for good. It now follows Android's download of Gemini Nano and checks again every 30 seconds, and until it's there, the open model answers if you've downloaded it.
  • The open model answers the first time. It loads as soon as it's the one in use, so your first question isn't spent waiting for it, and the Car Thing gives it longer to answer.

Changed

  • Wisp's voice is on from the start, saying what your voice commands did, a little quicker at 1.1x. Change it in Settings → Wisp's voice; if you turn it off, it stays off.
  • Update the Wisp app too (1.0.2, from inside the app): the thinking fixes are in it.

Try it: say "Okay Wisp, tell me a fun fact about the moon." Only the model on your phone can answer that.
