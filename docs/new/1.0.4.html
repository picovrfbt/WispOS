Wisp 1.0.4

Updates take care of themselves now, on the Car Thing and on your phone.

How to update What came in 1.0.3
Updates

Wisp keeps itself up to date

A new Wisp downloads through your phone and is there the next time the Car Thing starts. The Wisp app updates itself too.

How to update ›

Changed

  • The Car Thing updates itself. A new Wisp installs while you drive, without taking over the screen, and is there the next time it starts. Turn it off in Settings → Software update.
  • The Wisp app updates itself too. It checks every few hours and installs new versions by itself where Android allows it. Turn it off in the app: Options → Update the app automatically.
  • When an update needs you, the Wisp app says so with a notification you can tap to update, and a pop-up when you open it.

Fixed

  • The Wisp link comes back by itself after the app updates and after your phone restarts, so the Car Thing finds it without opening the app.

This is the last update you'll need to install by hand: update the Car Thing (Settings → Software update) and the Wisp app (from inside it) once more, and they keep themselves current from here. (Since 1.0.5 the Car Thing asks first again, unless you tick the box to install updates automatically.)
