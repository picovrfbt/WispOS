What's new · October 18, 2026

Wisp 1.0.2

Fixes from the first rides with passengers picking the music, and voice commands back on Car Things that came to Wisp OS from an older Wisp.

How to update What came in 1.0.1

Fixed

  • Voice commands work again. On a Car Thing flashed with Wisp OS over an older Wisp, the old settings pointed at a speech model Wisp OS doesn't have, so nothing you said got through. Wisp now uses the one that's there, and turns on the smarter voice those old settings left off. No reflash needed.
  • Saving the passengers' songs works. Spotify kept turning the playlist away; Wisp now makes it the way Spotify's own apps do.
  • The QR code shows right away when passengers pick the music, instead of sometimes waiting on "Starting…".
  • The passengers' page fits their phones. Long YouTube Music results no longer push it wider than the screen.

Behind the scenes

  • Wisp's own relay carries passengers' messages, so a long trip with lots of searching doesn't run into a daily limit. The free public one is still there as a backup.
  • Fewer messages go out for each session, so it all goes further.
