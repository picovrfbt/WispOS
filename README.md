  YouTube Music and SoundCloud, and music that follows how you drive.

## ✨ What it does

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🎵 <b>Your library, on the knob</b></td>
    <td>Recents, Liked Songs, Spotify's DJ, playlists, albums and artists. Turn to browse, press to play, start from any song or shuffle.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🎤 <b>Lyrics that keep time</b></td>
    <td>Synced lyrics beside the cover, word by word where available, for Spotify and for other players on your phone.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🗺️ <b>Directions on the dash</b></td>
    <td>Turn-by-turn from Google Maps on your phone, on a live map with the route drawn, your speed, the next turn and when you'll arrive. Share a place to Wisp to see the whole trip.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🗣️ <b>"Okay Wisp"</b></td>
    <td>Say what to play from your library, or on YouTube Music or SoundCloud. Queue it, like it, add it to a playlist, skip, pause or change the volume. Wisp can answer back through your phone.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">📻 <b>More than Spotify</b></td>
    <td>YouTube Music and SoundCloud libraries on Home, with up next and likes, plus whatever else your phone is playing.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🚗 <b>Drive Mode</b> <i>(beta)</i></td>
    <td>Music that opens up as you drive: it follows your speed, pulling away and braking. Three sounds: Ethereal, Cinematic and Lo-fi.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">📞 <b>Calls and notifications</b></td>
    <td>Answer or decline calls with the knob. Your phone's notifications appear as banners, and its battery shows when it's low.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🌈 <b>Visualizers</b></td>
    <td>A glow, bars, wave or particles behind the cover, or full screen, moving to the music playing on your phone.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🌙 <b>Made for the car</b></td>
    <td>Auto brightness, night mode from sunset to sunrise, a clock and weather screensaver, display size and accent colours.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">🔘 <b>Your buttons</b></td>
    <td>Each top button plays a playlist, or opens Home, the map, Drive Mode or voice, or plays, skips or likes. Hold one to save what's playing to it.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">⬆️ <b>Updates itself</b></td>
    <td>New versions download through your phone and install over the air, falling back on their own if something goes wrong.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">📱 <b>Set it from your phone</b></td>
    <td>Brightness, night mode, lyrics, Wisp's voice and more, from a page in the Wisp app. Changes show on the Car Thing right away.</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="30%">💾 <b>Never starts over</b></td>
    <td>Your settings are backed up to your phone and come back after a reflash or a reset.</td>
  </tr>
</table>

## 🚀 Getting started

### What you need

- A Spotify Car Thing
- A PC (Windows, Mac or Linux) and a USB-C cable that carries **data**, not just charge
- An Android phone for the Wisp app (Spotify on the Car Thing works without it)
- A Spotify account

> Flashing replaces the Car Thing's software. It's very hard to brick: if anything goes wrong, put it back in flash mode and start again.

### 1. Flash Wisp

1. Download the newest `wisp_firmware_vX.Y.Z.zip` from the [releases](../../releases) (not every release has one; any will do, since Wisp updates itself afterwards). Leave it zipped.
2. **On Windows only**, install the USB driver for the flasher first. In PowerShell:
   ```powershell
   irm https://driver.terbium.app/get | iex
   ```
3. Hold **buttons 1 and 4** (the first and last buttons along the top) while you plug the Car Thing into the PC. A black screen means it's ready.
4. Open [Terbium](https://terbium.app) in Chrome or Edge and follow its steps.
5. At **Choose your firmware**, pick **Local archive** and choose the zip.
6. Wait 5–10 minutes. If it stops or stalls for several minutes, start again from step 3.
7. When it's done, unplug the Car Thing and plug it back in.

The first start takes a few minutes, until the Car Thing asks how to connect.

### 2. Get it online

The Car Thing has no Wi-Fi, so it borrows your phone's or your PC's internet. The QR codes on the Car Thing's connect screens open these steps.

#### Over Bluetooth, from your phone

1. Turn on **Bluetooth tethering** on your phone first.
   - Android: Settings → Connections → Mobile Hotspot and Tethering → Bluetooth tethering. Android turns it off again whenever Bluetooth goes off.
   - iPhone: Settings → Personal Hotspot → Allow Others to Join. iPhones share mobile data only, never Wi-Fi.
2. Tap **Connect with Bluetooth** on the Car Thing.
3. Pick **Wisp** in your phone's Bluetooth list and check the codes on both screens match.

#### Over USB, from a PC

Tap **Connect to PC** on the Car Thing, then share your PC's internet with it:

- **Windows:** Win+R → `ncpa.cpl`. Right-click your internet adapter → Properties → Sharing → tick **Allow other network users…** and pick the Car Thing's adapter (the one described as *Remote NDIS*).
- **Mac:** System Settings → General → Sharing → Internet Sharing, sharing to the Car Thing's USB interface.
- **Linux:** share the connection in NetworkManager, using the Car Thing's `enx…` interface (its address starts `a0:b1:c2`):
  ```bash
  sudo nmcli connection add type ethernet ifname enxXXXX con-name carthing ipv4.method shared ipv6.method ignore
  sudo nmcli connection up carthing
  ```

### 3. Sign in to Spotify

Scan the QR code on the Car Thing with your phone and sign in. Play something anywhere on your Spotify account and it shows up.

### 4. Install the Wisp app (Android)

1. Download `wisp-companion-vX.Y.Z.apk` from the [latest release](../../releases/latest) on your phone and install it (allow installs from your browser when Android asks).
2. Open it and follow the setup steps; each one says what it's for and you can skip any.

The app updates itself from here on, and **Settings → Software update** on the Car Thing keeps Wisp up to date.

### If something's not right

| What you see | Try |
| --- | --- |
| Stuck on "starting up" for over a minute | Unplug and plug the Car Thing back in, and check the phone's tethering or the PC's sharing is still on |
| No pairing prompt on the phone | Turn Bluetooth tethering on **before** pairing, then try again |
| Lost internet over USB after unplugging | Windows turns sharing off when the Car Thing resets: tick it again in `ncpa.cpl` |
| The app can't find the Car Thing | Make sure the phone is the one tethering it, and the app's setup steps are all done |

Still stuck? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose).

## 🛠️ For developers

| Folder | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [`wisp-daemon`](wisp-daemon) | Go daemon (a go-librespot fork): Spotify Connect, library, the phone link, voice, the updater |

| [`wisp-firmware`](wisp-firmware) | Void Linux image builder (Docker) |

<details>
<summary><b>Releases and over-the-air updates</b></summary>

<br>

A release `vX.Y.Z` carries the flashable image (`wisp_firmware_vX.Y.Z.zip`)
and the app bundle Wisp updates itself from (`wisp-app-vX.Y.Z.tar.gz` plus its
`.sha256`). On the device, Settings → Software update installs a newer bundle
into the writable data partition and falls back to the flashed version if it
doesn't come up. The repo is private, so the companion app passes Wisp a
read-only GitHub token over the Bluetooth tether.

To cut a release, in `wisp-firmware` (WSL/Linux):

```sh
IMAGE_VERSION=vX.Y.Z just docker-run      # image, includes `just prepare`
just app-bundle vX.Y.Z                     # output/wisp-app-vX.Y.Z.tar.gz(.sha256)
gh release create vX.Y.Z output/wisp-app-vX.Y.Z.tar.gz* --notes "- what changed"
```

Updates cover the daemon and the UI. Changes to the system itself (services,
kernel, boot logos) need a flash of the image.

</details>
